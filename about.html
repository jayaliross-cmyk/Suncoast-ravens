About Us

Who We Are

The Suncoast Ravens are more than a baseball team. We are a family. We are leaders. We are committed to developing young men on and off the field.

Founded by a United States Navy veteran, our program is built on discipline, leadership, character, and service — teaching young athletes that who they become matters as much as how they play.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an environment where character, discipline, and hard work build champions for life. We prepare young athletes to compete with confidence and represent the Ravens the right way — on the field, at school, and in the community.

One team. One flock. One mission.

What We Stand For

  • Discipline and accountability, on and off the field
  • Leadership development through team sport
  • Family-first culture and community involvement
  • Competitive travel baseball at a high standard

Serving Central Florida

Suncoast Ravens is a youth travel baseball organization proudly serving families throughout Central Florida, including Ocoee, Minneola, Groveland, and Orlando. We field coach pitch and travel-level teams for young athletes looking to develop real baseball skills in a program built on discipline, character, and family.

Whether you're searching for a travel baseball team near Minneola, a coach pitch program in Groveland, or a youth baseball organization in Ocoee or Orlando, Suncoast Ravens welcomes players and families from across the region to join the Flock.
